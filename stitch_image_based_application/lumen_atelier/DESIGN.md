## Brand & Style

The design system embodies an editorial haute-couture atmosphere fused with modern computational intelligence. It targets discerning fashion consumers, art directors, and modern aesthetes who appreciate architectural tailoring, understated luxury, and quiet digital sophistication.

The emotional signature is poised, serene, and deliberate. The visual language merges high-end print publishing with restrained digital interaction. Instead of intrusive algorithmic novelty, AI elements appear as an invisible master tailor—discreet, discerning, and context-aware.

### Design Principles
- **Editorial Restraint:** Generous negative space, calibrated hierarchy, and gallery-like composition. Interfaces defer to garment photography.
- **Architectural Precision:** Structural discipline with razor-sharp alignments, subtle structural divider lines, and quiet tactile feedback.
- **Synthesized Intelligence:** Generative features and AI curation feel bespoke and quiet rather than synthetic or playful, rendered through luminous glass surfaces and hairline accents.

## Colors

The palette draws inspiration from atelier raw materials: hand-loomed parchment, unbleached cotton, mineral stones, and deep sumi ink.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#121212` - Deep Charcoal):** Grounding ink tone used for primary typography, dominant structural frames, and definitive interactions. Never pure digital black.
- **Neutral (`#F9F8F5` - Ivory Alabaster):** Warm, tactile canvas background that eliminates harsh glare while enhancing fabric textures.
- **Secondary (`#766E65` - Warm Stone):** Mid-tone for supporting metadata, editorial subheadings, and secondary actions.
- **Tertiary (`#2B3A42` - Slate Steel):** Understated cool contrast tone reserved for algorithmic intelligence indicators, virtual dressing room surfaces, and interactive tailoring states.

### Accent & AI Signifiers
- **AI Surface Tint:** `rgba(43, 58, 66, 0.04)` to `rgba(43, 58, 66, 0.08)` for container surfaces.
- **Luminescent Accent:** A crisp, high-refraction pearl glow (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`) on hover triggers over deep surfaces.
- **Surface Borders:** `rgba(18, 18, 18, 0.08)` across standard components; hairline crispness throughout.

## Typography

The typographic system creates an interplay between `Newsreader` (an editorial serif with literary warmth and sharp terminal curves) and `Hanken Grotesk` (a contemporary geometric sans-serif engineered for crystalline technical clarity).

### Usage Rules
- All major headings (`display-hero`, `headline-lg`, `headline-md`) must render in italic or regular `Newsreader` to evoke the intimacy of printed monographs.
- Functional data, product sizing, composition tables, navigation items, and AI metadata require `Hanken Grotesk`.
- `label-caps` enforces uppercase styling for micro-tags, SKUs, and category markers.
- Never use bold weights for editorial serifs; hierarchy must stem from scale, posture (italic vs. upright), and tonal contrast.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a disciplined 12-column editorial grid inspired by Swiss poster design and modern art monographs.

### Form Factors & Adaptation
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1.5rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Product listings cascade in deliberate single or staggered asymmetrical pairs.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout with `3rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Editorial hero modules shift to balanced 4:4 splits.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout with max-width `1600px`, `5rem` horizontal margins, and `2.5rem` gutters. Ample whitespace framing keeps garments centered like museum artifacts.

### Composition Rhythms
- Sections rely on vertical pauses of `space-2xl` to separate curation narratives, styling suggestions, and gallery grids.
- Asymmetrical column spans (e.g., 7 columns for imagery paired with 5 columns for AI curation commentary) break standard e-commerce monotony.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids heavy drop shadows, opting instead for architectural planes defined by low-contrast outlines, tonal layering, and optical glass surfaces.

### Depth Paradigms
- **Base Canvas:** Solid ivory ground (`#F9F8F5`).
- **Surface Elevation 1 (Card/Container):** Crisp 1px outline of `rgba(18, 18, 18, 0.07)` over a flat surface. In dark mode or inverted sections, uses `#1A1A1A`.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (Floating Cart / AI Drawer / Quick View):** `#FDFDFD` with a diffuse ambient penumbra: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(18, 18, 18, 0.05)`, flanked by an ultra-thin border (`rgba(18, 18, 18, 0.08)`).
- **AI Glass Planes:** Translucent layering using `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)` over `rgba(249, 248, 245, 0.82)` with an inner rim highlight of `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`.

## Shapes

The design system uses architectural, razor-sharp forms (`roundedness: 0`). 

Rectilinear geometry mirrors bespoke textile bolts, photographic framing, and physical print publications. 
- Form fields, product image containers, dialogs, drawers, and buttons all maintain precise 90-degree corners.
- The sole exception to this geometric discipline occurs in compact AI status pips and circular color swatch selectors, which remain pure geometric circles (`border-radius: 9999px`) to create intentional contrast against the otherwise rectilinear canvas.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#121212` background, white label in `label-caps`, 0px border radius. Padding: `1rem 2rem`. State changes are pure value shifts: hover deepens to `#262626` with subtle inner tracking expansion.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent base with a 1px border in `#121212`. Hover inverts background to `#121212` with text shifting to `#F9F8F5`.
- **AI Action:** Hairline border in `rgba(43, 58, 66, 0.4)` with an ambient background of `rgba(43, 58, 66, 0.04)`. Accompanied by a 4-point starlet or luminescent status dot.

### AI Curation & Stylist Badges
- **LUMEN Lens Badge:** A structured micro-chip displaying `label-ai`. Features an inverted color scheme (`#121212` text over `rgba(43, 58, 66, 0.08)` background) paired with a 1px border in `rgba(43, 58, 66, 0.25)`.
- **Fit & Draping Prediction Tag:** Positioned at the top corner of garment cards; non-intrusive micro-tag featuring an ultra-subtle pulsing optic dot (1.5s ease breath).

### Product Cards
- **Structure:** Frameless image container with an aspect ratio of `3:4` or `4:5`. Background fill uses `#F2EFE9` for placeholder loading.
- **Content Rhythm:** Product title in `headline-sm` (Newsreader), price and fabric origin in `body-sm` (Hanken Grotesk). AI sizing recommendation surfaces as a delicate inline annotation underneath the price.
- **Hover Micro-interaction:** Garment transitions smoothly from studio still to dynamic drape/movement video with a gentle 400ms ease curve; action links reveal themselves via vertical offset rather than aggressive overlays.

### Inputs & Selectors
- **Input Fields:** Flat bottom border only (`1px solid rgba(18, 18, 18, 0.2)`), transparent background, label positioned in `label-caps`. Focus state transitions the border to `#121212` with no outer glow.
- **Radio & Size Buttons:** Rectangular matrix of border-only tiles (`1px solid rgba(18, 18, 18, 0.12)`). Selected state turns solid `#121212` with white text. Disabled/out-of-stock sizes show a clean diagonal hairline slash.
- **Checkboxes:** Square 16px boxes, unrounded. Checked state renders a solid fill with an ivory interior indicator.

### Editorial Lists & Wardrobe Tables
- Border-separated modular rows using `1px solid rgba(18, 18, 18, 0.08)`. Columns distribute sizing, composition, generative styling suggestions, and inventory availability with typographic hierarchy rather than heavy icon indicators.
